  <h1>Create a new panel in OrderConnect</h1>

</div>

1. Navigate to the OrderConnect Admin Portal at https://orderconnect.spectrumhealth.org/admin.
2. Click the **Panels** tab on the menu at the bottom of the screen.

## Add Panel form

3. Click the **Add** button at the top-right of the screen.
4. In the **Panel Name** field, enter a name for the new panel (e.g., "Test Panel").
5. In the **Panel Description** field, enter a description for the new panel (e.g., "This is a test panel.").
6. Click *Next*

## Edit Orders in the Panel form

7. Type the *Order Code* of the order you want to add to the panel (e.g., "12345") in the **Order Code** field.
8. Click the **Search** button.

The order should be displayed in the *Resultant Orders* section.

9. Click the order in the *Resultant Orders* section to select it,
10. Click the **>** button to move the order from the *Resultant Orders* section to the *Orders in Panel* section.

Repeat these steps for all Order Codes.

Once all Order Codes have been added:

11. Click the **Save** button at the bottom of the form to save the new panel.
