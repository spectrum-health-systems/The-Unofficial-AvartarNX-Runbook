  <h1>Creating a new Agency/Clinic in OrderConnect</h1>

</div>

1. Navigate to the OrderConnect Admin Portal at https://orderconnect.spectrumhealth.org/admin.

2. Click the **Agencies** tab on the menu at the bottom of the screen.

3. Click the **Add New Agency (Clinic)** button at the top of the screen.

4. Complete the following fields in the form:

   - Agency Name
   - Address
   - City
   - State
   - Zip Code
   - Phone Number

5. Leave the following fields at their default values:

   - DSM Description
   - RxInform Enabled
   - Allow Changing Route for Medications
   - Require Diagnosis for Medication
   - Output Template

6. In the **Default Laboratory Destination** dropdown, select ***Quest Labs***.

7. Click the **Save** button at the bottom of the form, and the new agency/clinic will now be created and added to the list of agencies in OrderConnect.

8. Copy the **AgencyID** value that was assigned to the new agency/clinic.

9. Click the **Agency name** of the new agency/clinic to open the *Agency Edit Webpage Dialog*.

10. Click the **(Add)** text next to ***Destinations*** in the ***Desitinations Associated*** section.

11. In the **Destination** dropdown, select ***Quest Labs***.

12. Paste the ***AgencyID*** into the **Agency Client ID** field.

13. Click the **Save** text to the right of **Agency Client ID** field.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Make sure you click the Save **text** and not the Save **button**!

14. Click the **Save** button at the bottom of the *Agency Edit Webpage Dialog* to save the changes to the new agency/clinic.
